My Background

Developer by Profession, Teacher by Passion.

Safa Oujri is a state engineer in computer science and networks (EMSI, 2022-2025) with a MIAGE specialization. She also holds a DEUG in physical sciences (FSBM, Casablanca, 2020-2022) and a baccalaureate in physical sciences (Lycée Sidi Salah, Zagora, 2019-2020). She is looking for a pre-employment internship where she can contribute to concrete projects and grow within a technical team, drawing on her skills in application development, DevOps, and security. Her personal strengths include a strong sense of commitment and responsibility, resilience and adaptability, and creativity combined with an analytical mindset.

Professionally, she completed her final-year internship at Yokamos Group (March-July 2025, Casablanca), where she designed and deployed a multi-client Security Operations Center (SOC) in MSSP mode based on Wazuh. She configured security event monitoring and detection, implemented client isolation and access management, and built custom dashboards for alert tracking (Wazuh, OpenSearch, Docker, Java, Spring Boot, REST API, ReactJS, Linux). During her PFA internship at Vinci Energies Afrique de l’Ouest (July-September 2024), she developed a web application for stock management and tracking, featuring inventory entries and exits, automatic restocking alerts, and consumption reports (TEEPEE, Power BI).

On the technical side, she is proficient in many languages (C, C++, Java, Python, JavaScript, PHP, SQL, TypeScript, Dart) and frameworks (Spring Boot, Spring Cloud, Django, ASP.NET, Laravel, Node.js, Angular, React, React Native, Flutter), as well as DevOps and cloud tools (Git, GitLab, Docker, Linux, CI/CD, AWS, Kubernetes, OpenShift). She also has skills in ERP (Odoo, SAP), BI and data warehousing, security (Wazuh, JWT/OAuth2), modeling (Merise, UML), and artificial intelligence. Her academic projects show this versatility: an intelligent e-learning platform with AI and serious games, the TomorZagora e-commerce application, an HR decision-support solution with Power BI, and the DevSearch application. She holds certifications in DevOps, AWS, Google Cloud IAM, Spring Boot, and SAP HANA, and speaks Arabic (native), French (fluent), English (fluent), and Spanish (intermediate).
